Non Profit Information

Bronze Information

cost: 100,000 FCFA

✅Bronze membership provides basic benefits to members.

✅ It is design for an operational member witch focus on maintaining continuity of business activities.

Silver Information

cost: 200,000 FCFA

✅Silver membership provides additional benefits to members.

✅ It is for the member that follow a Business Continuity and Incident Management Plan.

✅they are responsible for coordinating response efforts

Gold Information

cost: 300,000 FCFA

✅ Gold membership provides the highest level of membership benefits.

✅It is design for responsible for considering the long-term implications of an incident.
