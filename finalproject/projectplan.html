Project Plan

Site name: Extra Market plus

this market name represents a modern shopping experience with a wide variety of products and services.

Site purpose:

The purpose of the Extra Market plus website is to provide a comprehensive online shopping platform that offers a wide range of products and services to meet the diverse needs of customers.

Target audience:

The target audience for the Extra Market plus website includes individuals of all ages who are interested in online shopping and are looking for a convenient and reliable platform to purchase products and services. This includes tech-savvy individuals, busy professionals, and anyone seeking a seamless shopping experience.

Scenarios:

1. A busy professional looking for a convenient way to purchase groceries and household items online.

2. A tech-savvy individual seeking a wide variety of products and services, including electronics, fashion, and home goods.

3. A family looking for a reliable platform to purchase products and services for their household needs, including groceries, clothing, and entertainment.
